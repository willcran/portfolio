About

This app aggregates live and historical data from multiple motorsport APIs and presents it in a clean, easy-to-understand interface.

Starting with support for Formula 1, BTCC, and WEC, the app provides up-to-date information on current standings, race progress, and key session details.

Users can track live race information including race standings, track status updates such as red flags and penalties, and detailed insights into drivers, teams, and circuits.

By collecting data from multiple sources and surfacing it in a unified format, the app removes the need to jump between platforms and makes it easier to stay on top of everything happening across different championships.
