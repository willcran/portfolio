Docka (GNOME Dock)

A lightweight GNOME extension that reveals the dock with pressure at the bottom of the screen.

View on GitHub View on GNOME Extensions

About

Docka is a minimal GNOME Shell extension that enhances workflow by revealing the dock when pressure is applied to the bottom edge of the screen.

Designed for users who prefer a clean and distraction-free desktop, Docka keeps the dock hidden until it is needed, reducing visual clutter while maintaining quick access to applications.

Inspired by Dock Express by fthx, Docka focuses on simplicity and responsiveness, delivering a smooth and intuitive way to interact with the dock through edge-based gestures.
